About

I recently completed my MSc in Physics at the University of Milan (110/110 cum laude), with a thesis in the theory of continuously monitored open quantum systems. My work sits at the intersection of quantum information, quantum optics, and quantum metrology: I study how the information gained by continuously measuring an open system can be used to engineer useful quantum states and to sharpen parameter estimation.

I also enjoy the computational side of the field, from stochastic simulations in Python and QuTiP to lower-level work in C++. Before returning to research I spent two years teaching computer science, which taught me that I understand an idea only once I can explain it plainly.

Interests

03

MSc thesis

Master's thesis

Entangling Unravellings for Quantum State Engineering and Quantum Metrology

Supervisors: Prof. Marco G. Genoni · Dr. Eoin O'Connor — University of Milan

The thesis studies how different continuous-measurement strategies, or unravellings, of an open quantum system's dynamics can generate entanglement and spin squeezing, and quantifies the resulting metrological advantage through the quantum Fisher information.
