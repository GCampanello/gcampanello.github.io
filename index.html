Projects

Expected value and risk in 10eLotto

71.1% best return to player among all 60 possible bets, so no bet has a positive expected value

10eLotto is an Italian keno game with a draw every five minutes. I derived the exact payout distribution of every possible bet from the hypergeometric law of the draws and the optional Numero Oro, Doppio Oro and Extra add-ons, then checked it with Monte Carlo simulations, which also show how quickly a budget runs out. Tests on 768 real draws, with the uniformity test corrected for sampling without replacement, find no departure from chance: numbers that have not come out for a while are no more likely than the others.

Python (NumPy, SciPy, pandas, Matplotlib), web scraping, LaTeX

Take-home pay and long-run wealth under Italian tax rules

67.2% effective marginal rate at €38k gross: of each extra euro, 33 cents reach the employee

A model of an employee's pay in Milan under the 2026 rules: social security contributions, income tax brackets and deductions, including the tax-wedge cut, and regional and municipal surcharges. It maps the effective marginal rate, which stays above 55% between about €35k and €44k of gross salary, measures the fiscal drag caused by brackets that do not follow inflation, and projects savings over 30 years with a Monte Carlo of lognormal equity returns and AR(1) inflation.

Python (NumPy, pandas, Matplotlib)

Physics background

My MSc thesis was in quantum information theory, on open quantum systems watched by a continuous measurement. In practice it meant stochastic calculus, simulation and estimation from noisy data, the same tools I want to use next.

Entangling Unravellings for Quantum State Engineering and Quantum Metrology

Supervisors: Prof. Marco G. Genoni and Dr. Eoin O’Connor
University of Milan

The thesis studies how different ways of continuously measuring an open quantum system, called unravellings, can entangle two distant qubits and generate spin squeezing, and how much they improve the precision of parameter estimation, measured by the quantum Fisher information. Simulations in Python with QuTiP, built on stochastic master equations and quantum trajectories.
